Company Overview

Thirty Years of Engineering Excellence

Established in 1995, Kurlekar Engineering Works is a professionally managed manufacturing unit in Malad West, Mumbai. For over three decades, we have specialised in precision sheet metal press components — building deep tooling expertise and a reputation for consistent, reliable supply.

We supply India's top OEM manufacturers across the electric motor, fan, and automotive sectors — manufacturing every component exactly as per customer drawings or approved samples.

Our strength lies in in-house die making, stringent process control, and a long-term partnership approach — giving customers one accountable partner from first article to volume delivery.
